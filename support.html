Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lucky12 free to use?

Yes. The App is free to download and contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscription fees. All features are unlocked out of the box.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Lucky12 works fully offline and does not require sign-in, registration, or any email address to use.

Where is my data stored?

All progress, scores, favorites, and your decorated photos are stored locally on your iPhone inside the App's private sandbox. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I back up or transfer my data?

Data is included in your standard iOS device or iCloud backup if iCloud Backup is enabled. To start fresh on a new device, simply reinstall the App.

Why does the App ask for Camera or Photo Library access?

Only the Photo Studio uses these permissions — to take a new photo or import/export one. You may decline and still use every other feature.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the App, go to Settings → Reset Progress, or simply delete and reinstall the App for a complete reset.

The Daily Card didn't update today — what should I do?

The Daily Card refreshes based on your device's local date at the first launch of a new day. Pull-to-refresh on the Home screen, or restart the App if it appears stuck.

The App is crashing or misbehaving — what should I check?

Please confirm your iPhone is running iOS 16 or later, restart the App, and reinstall if the issue persists. If it continues, contact us with the details below.
