Welcome

Lucky12 (also known as Zodiac Quest) is a single-player cultural companion that celebrates the twelve animal archetypes of the East Asian zodiac. It is built for iPhone, runs offline, and stores everything locally on your device — there is no sign-in, no wagering, no virtual currency, and no real-money play.

This site is the official support hub for the app. You can reach our team, read our privacy practices, or review the terms that govern your use of Lucky12.
