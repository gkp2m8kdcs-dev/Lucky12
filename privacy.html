App Name: Lucky12 · A Cultural Animal Companion

1. Overview

Lucky12 ("we", "our", "the App") is designed to operate privately on your iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how it is stored, and how you can contact us. By using Lucky12 you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any contact information inside the App.
  • We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertising networks, data brokers, or third-party analytics services.
  • The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it:

4. Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests only the permissions it needs to deliver the photo experience. You may decline any permission and continue using the rest of the App.

  • Camera — used only when you tap "Take Photo" inside the Photo Studio. The image is processed on-device.
  • Photo Library — used only when you tap "Import" or "Export" inside the Photo Studio.

5. Tracking Declaration

For purposes of Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, we make the following declarations regarding the Lucky12 application:

Does the App track users?

No. Lucky12 does not track users. We do not use tracking technologies, advertising identifiers (IDFA), fingerprinting, or any other mechanism to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

Does the App share data with third parties for tracking purposes?

No. Lucky12 does not share any user data with third parties for the purpose of tracking, advertising, attribution, or cross-app/cross-site behavioral analysis.

Does the App use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)?

No. The App does not access or use the IDFA or any equivalent identifier for advertising purposes.

Does the App perform any cross-app or cross-website tracking?

No. The App runs offline by default, has no social features, no sign-in, and does not link to external services that would enable cross-context tracking.

Because no tracking is performed and no tracking-related data is shared, Lucky12 does not display an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asking for "Allow Tracking" permission.

6. Children's Privacy

Lucky12 is appropriate for general audiences, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the App stores no personal data on a server and contains no advertising or social features, there is no children's data to disclose.

7. International Data Transfers

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data to our servers, there are no international data transfers of user information to disclose.

8. Your Rights

You have full control over the data stored by the App on your device. You may:

  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling Lucky12.
  • Revoke camera or photo-library permissions at any time from iOS Settings.
  • Contact us with questions or requests about your privacy.

9. Security

The App stores data within the iOS application sandbox, which is protected by iOS's built-in sandboxing. Because data is processed and stored only on your device, your information benefits from the same security features that protect the rest of your iPhone.
